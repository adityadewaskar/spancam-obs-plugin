Spancam for OBS

Use your Android phone or iPhone as a camera source in OBS Studio, over Wi‑Fi or USB, with no desktop helper app in between.

This installs one plugin into your OBS plugins folder. Nothing else on your Mac is touched, and no background service is added.

After installing:

  1. Quit OBS completely and open it again — it only scans for plugins at start.
  2. Choose Sources → + → Spancam Camera.
  3. The source shows a QR code to install the phone app if you have not yet.
